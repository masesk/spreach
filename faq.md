<details> 
    <summary>Does Spreach work without internet connection?</summary>
    Yes. All core features, including speech generation, document import, and camera scanning, work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to download the app, complete or restore a premium purchase, import a web article from a URL, and view the Terms of Use or Privacy Policy. The Desktop Companion needs a local Wi-Fi network shared with your computer, but not internet access.
</details> 
<details> 
    <summary>What can I import into Spreach?</summary>
    You can type or paste text, import PDF, EPUB, and plain text files, scan printed pages with your camera, choose photos from your library, or enter the address of a web article. Text from photos and scans is recognized on your device.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>How does importing a web article work?</summary>
    Enter the article's web address and Spreach loads the page on your device, finds the main article text, and reads it aloud. Your device connects directly to the website, just like a web browser would, so the website's own privacy policy applies. Spreach does not send the address or the article to us.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why is a web article missing text or including extra content?</summary>
    Every website is built differently, and Spreach extracts text automatically, so it may occasionally pick up menus or ads, or miss part of the article. Pages behind a login or paywall, or pages that load very slowly, may not import at all. If an article doesn't import well, you can copy its text and paste it into Spreach instead.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why is Spreach not detecting any text from my PDF?</summary>
    Spreach does not scan images within PDFs. To ensure your document imports successfully, it must contain selectable text. If a document lacks highlightable text, its content will not be recognized during the import process. For scanned documents, try taking photos of the pages with the camera import instead.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>What is the Desktop Companion?</summary>
    The Desktop Companion is an optional app for your computer (macOS, Windows, or Linux) that lets Spreach use your computer's processor or graphics card to generate audio, which can be faster than generating it on your iPhone. Your text is sent only to your own computer over your local network and never to our servers. The Desktop Companion is included with Spreach Premium.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>How do I connect to the Desktop Companion?</summary>
    Open the Desktop Companion on your computer and click 'Launch Service'. On your iPhone, go to Settings (gear icon), tap 'Desktop Companion', and select your computer from the list. When asked, allow Spreach to access your local network.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why can't my iPhone find or connect to the Desktop Companion?</summary>
    Make sure the Desktop Companion service is running and that your iPhone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest, public, and some office networks block devices from communicating with each other, so use a private home network. Also check that Local Network access is turned on for Spreach in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, and that your computer's firewall allows the Desktop Companion.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Is it safe to use the Desktop Companion on public Wi-Fi?</summary>
    We don't recommend it. While its service is running, the Desktop Companion is visible to other devices on the same network, and the connection between your iPhone and computer may not be encrypted. Only use it on a private network you trust, and shut down the service when you're not using it.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why is the Desktop Companion using storage on my computer?</summary>
    The Desktop Companion saves generated audio so it doesn't need to regenerate it. You can see how much space it's using and clear it at any time from the gear menu in the Desktop Companion by selecting 'Clear Saved Audios'.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>How does the free trial and Premium work?</summary>
    Spreach includes a 30-day free trial that starts after you complete onboarding. Once the trial ends, you can unlock Spreach Premium with a one-time purchase. It's not a subscription, so there are no recurring charges. Premium also unlocks the Desktop Companion. The trial can only be used once per device, and reinstalling the app does not restart it.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>I already bought Premium. How do I get it back on a new device or after reinstalling?</summary>
    Sign in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase, open the purchase screen, and tap 'Restore Purchases'. Refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?</summary>
    Your reading history, saved audio, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. You can clear your history from the sidebar or remove all saved data from Settings. Deleting the app also removes this data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why is the app crashing?</summary>
    Spreach is actively under development, and certain text inputs or character types may occasionally cause unexpected behavior. If you experience consistent crashes, please report them through our contact form so we can investigate and resolve the issue.
</details> 
<details> 
    <summary>Why is the app using so much storage?</summary>
    Spreach includes all necessary AI models directly within the app package. It also caches generated audio so you can replay content offline without regenerating it. You can free up space by clearing saved data in Settings. We are working on features to help reduce the overall storage footprint in future updates.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Why does the app crash or stop playing when I send it to the background?</summary>
    Spreach must remain open while processing and generating audio from your text. If you need to navigate away from the screen, you can safely minimize the app by tapping the More menu (...) at the top and selecting 'Background Mode'.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>Does Spreach support different themes such as dark theme?</summary>
    Yes, Spreach offers a variety of themes, each featuring a unique color palette and design. To customize your interface, tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner and navigate to the Themes section.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>How many people are working on the app?</summary>
    Spreach is currently maintained by a sole developer on a part-time basis. We appreciate your patience as we work to address inquiries and implement updates as efficiently as possible.
</details>
<details> 
    <summary>How does the magic work?</summary>
    Spreach leverages optimized, lightweight AI models, including the Kokoro 82M text-to-speech model, to generate high-fidelity, natural-sounding audio directly on your device or on your own computer.
</details>
